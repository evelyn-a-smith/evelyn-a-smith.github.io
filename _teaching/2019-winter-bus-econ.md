This course examines the development of analytical tools and their application to important economic issues. Refines central concepts concerning competition, economic efficiency and the function of property rights, and discusses the effects of the regulation and the rationale for government intervention in the economy. Other topics include the analysis of capital, uncertainty and monopoly.

I served as Instructor of Record for this course and was honored to receive the 2019 Gerald and Lillian Dykstra Fellowship, the highest honor awarded to graduate student instructors in the Ross School of Business.
