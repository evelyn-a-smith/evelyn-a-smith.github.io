This course covers essential tools and concepts in economics, including supply and demand, equilibrium, imperfect competition, basic game theory, and information asymmetries. The goal of the course is to help students better understand economic phenomena and the behavior of individuals and firms and the markets in which they operate.   

I served as a teaching assistant for this course under Professor Arik Levinson in the Fall of 2013.
